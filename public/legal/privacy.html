3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the service (saving, syncing, leaderboard): performance of the contract.
  • Activating purchased packs and keeping accounts: performance of the contract and legal obligation.
  • Security and cheat prevention: legitimate interest.
  • Service emails (address confirmation, forgotten password): performance of the contract. No marketing emails without your consent.

4. Retention period

  • Account and progress: as long as the account exists. Deleted immediately when you delete your account.
  • Purchase log (email, amount, reference): 10 years (the retention period for accounting records under OHADA law), detached from the account once it is deleted.

5. Recipients and processors

  • Supabase: database and authentication.
  • Chariow: payment processing and delivery of downloadable resources.
  • Vercel: hosting of the app.

These providers may process data outside your country; they are chosen for their security guarantees.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Deleting your account is done directly in the app (Shop › Settings › “Delete my account”). For any other request: bmug9094@gmail.com. Reply within 30 days.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can contact the personal data protection authority provided for by the Digital Code of the Democratic Republic of the Congo (Ordinance-Law No. 23/010 of 13 March 2023).

7. Minors

KaziBoss is intended for people aged 13 and over. Purchases by a minor must be made with a parent's consent.

8. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords never stored in plain text, and database-level security rules that limit each player's access to their own data. Packs can only be activated by our server after the payment signature has been verified.
