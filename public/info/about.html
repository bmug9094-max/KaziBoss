KaziBoss: become the boss of your business

KaziBoss is a free, gamified app that teaches market traders and small entrepreneurs in Africa how to manage their money. You play a 30-day shop simulation, follow quick lessons with quizzes, and use real management tools for your own business. It runs on any phone, even offline, in English and French.

Play for free

Who is it for?

  • Market traders, neighbourhood shopkeepers, street vendors and wholesalers.
  • Young entrepreneurs, students and training programmes (NGOs, cooperatives, microfinance) teaching small-business finance.
  • Built first for French-speaking Africa (DR Congo, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Cameroon, Benin, Togo, Burkina Faso, Mali, Guinea, Gabon, Congo-Brazzaville, Rwanda, Burundi, Madagascar…), usable anywhere.

The simulation: 30 days to keep your shop running

You open your shop with $100. Every day, a character brings you a real-life situation: a wholesaler offering a discount, a customer asking for credit, a power cut, a cousin asking for money, a fake Mobile Money SMS, the local currency sliding against the dollar… You swipe the card left or right to choose. Four gauges must stay above zero: cash, stock, reputation and energy. After each choice, a “Field Lesson” explains the financial principle in two sentences.

106 scenarios in total, including three unlockable sectors: Trade & Distribution, Services & Digital and Agri-Business & Processing. XP, levels, badges, daily streaks, a city leaderboard and KaziCoins keep learning motivating.

Lessons: 12 lessons, 56 quizzes

  1. Survival & Cash: personal vs business cash, the cash book, the safety cushion.
  2. Prices & Margins: the real cost price, margin vs markup, prices and exchange rates.
  3. Customers & Credit: safe customer credit, loyalty without discounts, Mobile Money and payment scams.
  4. Growth & Team (premium): hiring and paying staff, borrowing wisely (microcredit, savings circles, supplier credit), opening a second outlet.

Boss tools (free, offline)

  • Selling price calculator: cost price including transport, fixed costs and losses; recommended price from a target margin.
  • Exchange simulator: US dollar ↔ Congolese franc, CFA franc, Guinean franc, plus an inflation-protected price.
  • Cash book: money in, money out, personal withdrawals, daily balance and CSV export.

Pricing

The game, the first three modules and all the tools are free. Optional one-time packs (Mobile Money or card, via Chariow): Agri-Business & Import-Export Pack ($5), Unlimited Lives Pack ($3) and the Digital Growth Excel templates ($10).

Frequently asked questions

Is KaziBoss free?
Yes. The game, modules 1 to 3 and all the tools are free. Premium packs are optional one-time purchases ($3, $5 or $10).
Do I need to download KaziBoss from an app store?
No. Open www.kaziboss.com in your phone's browser and choose “Add to home screen”. The app then works offline.
Does KaziBoss work without internet?
Yes, after the first visit. An account is only needed to save progress across phones, join the leaderboard or buy a pack.
Which countries and currencies does it cover?
KaziBoss is designed for French-speaking Africa, with examples in US dollars and Congolese francs, and an exchange simulator for the Congolese franc (CDF), CFA franc (XAF/XOF) and Guinean franc (GNF).
Is it financial advice?
No. KaziBoss is educational: figures are realistic approximations to teach principles, not personalised advice.
How do I pay for a premium pack?
With Mobile Money or a bank card, through the secure payment provider Chariow. The pack is activated on your account as soon as payment is confirmed.
